B. Client Self-Service Portal (REVENUE MULTIPLIER)

Right now, clients (companies like Sapura) have zero visibility into their training history, invoices, or certificates. A client portal would:

  • Reduce admin workload by 30-40% (no more "can you resend the invoice?" emails)
  • Enable clients to download invoices, certificates, and training records themselves
  • Create a sticky relationship (clients depend on the portal)
  • Differentiate MDDRC from competitors who still use manual processes

C. Certificate Verification (TRUST & COMPLIANCE)

A public QR-code-based certificate verification page (e.g., mddrc.online/verify/CERT-12345) would:

  • Prevent certificate fraud
  • Add credibility for clients presenting to DOSH/JKKP/HRDC
  • Serve as organic marketing (every scan = brand exposure)

D. Automated Compliance Reporting

If MDDRC could auto-generate HRDC claim forms, DOSH compliance reports, or SST summaries, it would save hours of manual work and reduce errors.

3. The Buyer's Perspective

"If I were evaluating this system for purchase or investment, here's what would impress me — and what would concern me."

3.1 What Impresses

Financial Module Depth: The double-entry accounting with auto-journal posting, AR aging, P&L, General Ledger, marketing/trainer/payroll subledgers, and YoY comparison is enterprise-grade. Most training management systems don't touch this. This alone is a major differentiator.
Marketing Pipeline: Lead pipeline management with quotation workflows, client management, and commission tracking built directly into the training platform. This eliminates the need for a separate CRM.
Operational Completeness: Attendance, checklists, feedback, test management, vehicle details, indemnity forms, session costing — covers the actual day-to-day operations, not just the admin layer.
Data Management & Audit: SuperAdmin override capabilities with mandatory reason logging, audit trails, and backup/export functionality shows mature thinking about data governance.

3.2 What Concerns Me (as a Buyer)

No Email/Communication Integration: A system this comprehensive with ZERO automated email/WhatsApp notifications feels incomplete. I'd question whether the team can actually operate efficiently without constant manual follow-ups.
No Client-Facing Portal: If I'm a client company paying RM 50K+ annually for training, I expect a portal to view my invoices, download certificates, and track my employees' training status. Without this, MDDRC looks like an internal tool, not a service platform.
Certificate Generation is Manual: Certificates exist in the system but require manual upload. For a training company doing hundreds of sessions, this doesn't scale. Auto-generation from templates is essential.
No Multi-Tenancy: The system is built for MDDRC only. If the goal is to sell this as SaaS to other training providers, the architecture needs tenant isolation. If it's internal-only, this is fine for now.
Test Coverage: 23 automated tests for 491 endpoints means ~4.7% coverage. For a financial system handling invoices and payroll, this is risky. Critical financial flows should have 80%+ coverage.
No 2FA / Advanced Security: Rate limiting exists, but there's no two-factor authentication. For a system with financial data, payroll, and client information, 2FA should be standard.
