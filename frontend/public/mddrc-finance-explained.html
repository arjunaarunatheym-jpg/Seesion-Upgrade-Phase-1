1. The Big Picture — Two Accounting Engines

Your system has two independent ways of calculating financial data. Understanding this is key to reading your reports correctly.

Engine A: CEO P&L (Cash-Based)

  • Reads directly from raw data collections
  • Invoices, payments, session costs, petty cash
  • Simple: Revenue = Issued Invoices
  • Shows session profitability
  • Does NOT include payroll overhead
  • Best for: "Is this session profitable?"

Engine B: Auditor P&L (Journal-Based)

  • Reads ONLY from double-entry journal entries
  • Proper accounting: every debit has a credit
  • Includes ALL expenses (session + payroll + overhead)
  • Shows true company P&L
  • Powers the Balance Sheet
  • Best for: "Is the company profitable?"
Why do the net profit numbers differ?

CEO P&L: RM 8,055 (revenue minus session costs only). Auditor P&L: RM -7,462 (includes RM 15,517 payroll overhead). The difference IS the payroll. Both are correct — they answer different questions.

5. Reports — Which One to Read When

CEO P&L (Session Profitability)

Training Revenue (all issued/paid invoices)
− Trainer Fees
− Coordinator Fees
− Marketing Commissions
− Petty Cash
= Session Net Profit (your "margin per session")

Use this to answer: "Am I making money on each training?"

Auditor P&L (Full Company P&L)

Training Revenue (from journal entries)
− Cost of Sales (Trainer + Coordinator + Marketing + Direct costs)
= Gross Profit
− Operating Expenses (Payroll: Salary + EPF + SOCSO + EIS + Petty Cash)
= Net Profit

Use this to answer: "Is my company profitable after paying ALL bills?"

Balance Sheet (Financial Position)

ASSETS (what you own)
  Cash at Bank + Petty Cash + Accounts Receivable

= LIABILITIES (what you owe)
  Accounts Payable + SST + EPF + SOCSO + EIS + Deferred Revenue

+ EQUITY (retained earnings from P&L)

If Assets ≠ Liabilities + Equity → something is wrong → click Sync

6. The Sync System — How & When to Use

Where is it?

Finance Dashboard > Accounting Tab > "Sync Historical Transactions"

Two options:

ButtonWhat It DoesWhen to Use
Sync Historical Scans ALL raw data, creates ONLY missing journal entries. Non-destructive. After adding trainer fees, coordinator fees, session expenses, manual income/expenses. Safe to run anytime.
Reset & Re-sync Voids ALL existing auto-generated journals, then recreates everything from scratch. When numbers look wrong and you want a clean slate. Also fixes misclassified entries (e.g., Deferred Revenue → Revenue).
Recommended workflow:

1. Complete session costing (save trainer fees, coordinator fees, marketing commission)
2. Go to Finance > Accounting tab
3. Click "Sync Historical Transactions"
4. All data flows into your Auditor P&L and Balance Sheet automatically

7. About Your Diagnostic (4 TF/CF/MC Needing Fix)

The diagnostic found 4 journal entries for trainer fees / marketing commissions that were created with an older format (missing proper source_id references). This means the backfill can't match them to prevent duplicates.

How to fix:

  1. Go to Finance > Accounting tab
  2. Click "Reset & Re-sync"
  3. This will void the 4 old entries and recreate them properly with correct references
  4. After reset, the diagnostic should show 0 needing fix
