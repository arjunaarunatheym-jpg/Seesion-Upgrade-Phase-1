User Role Experience Audit

Trainee, Trainer, Coordinator & Supervisor Perspectives

Prepared: 2 April 2026 | MDDRC Training Management System

What's Good, What Needs Improving, What's Missing

1. As a Trainee (Participant)

"I'm Aini, a Sapura employee attending a 2-day Defensive Driving Course. I've been given login credentials and told to go to this system. Here's my experience..."

1.1 What I See

6 tabs: Overview (training info, attendance, vehicle details), Details (personal info), Certificates, Tests (pre-test, post-test), Checklists (vehicle inspection), Settings (password).

1.2 What's Good

  • First-time verification flow: On first login, the system guides me to verify my name, IC, email, and phone. Then asks me to accept the indemnity form. Smart onboarding.
  • Pre-test and post-test system: I can take tests directly from my dashboard. Results are shown immediately. Practical for measuring learning outcomes.
  • Vehicle details capture: I can enter my vehicle model, registration, and road tax expiry. This data feeds into the checklist system.
  • Clock in/out attendance: Digital attendance replaces paper sign-in. Good audit trail.
  • Certificate download: Once issued, I can download my certificate directly. No need to chase the admin.
  • Social media prompt: After completing feedback, the system suggests following MDDRC's social pages. Smart marketing touch.

1.3 What Needs Improving

2. As a Trainer

"I'm Faizal, a freelance trainer contracted by MDDRC. I conduct 3-4 sessions a month across different companies. Here's how the system works for me..."

2.1 What I See

7 tabs: Sessions (current assignments), Session Management (access control, participant management), Checklists (vehicle inspection forms), Past Training (history), My Earnings (income tracking), My Payroll (payslips, EA form), Feedback (chief trainer only).

2.2 What's Good

  • Session Management tab is powerful: I can release/disable pre-tests, post-tests, feedback, and checklists for participants. Full control over training flow.
  • Auto-distributed participant assignments: Participants are automatically split equally between trainers. No manual assignment needed.
  • Checklist system: I can fill out vehicle safety checklists per participant directly from my dashboard. Very practical for field use.
  • Earnings visibility: I can see my training income broken down by session. Transparent and builds trust.
  • Payroll integration: My payslips and EA form are accessible directly. No need for separate HR queries.
  • Chief vs Regular trainer distinction: Different capabilities based on role (chief trainer can submit feedback, view results summary).
  • Past training history with month/year filter: I can review any previous session's details and participant data.

2.3 What Needs Improving

IssueImpactSuggestion
Tab bar overflows horizontally (7 tabs)On mobile, tabs are cut off. Scrolling required.Use a dropdown menu or bottom nav for mobile. Priority tabs visible, others in "More".
No quick view of today's scheduleI have to scan through all sessions to find today'sAdd "Today's Session" summary card at the top - company name, location, participants count, start time.
Checklist form needs scrolling on mobileDuring practical training, filling checklists on a phone is tediousOptimize checklist form for one-handed mobile use (larger checkboxes, swipe gestures)
No participant photo / faceWith 15+ participants, I can't match names to facesAllow participant profile photos (even basic ones from verification step)
Earnings only show trainer incomeIf I'm also a coordinator, I need to switch context to see that incomeAlready handled for dual-role users (coordinator/marketing income tabs) - just ensure the UX is clear

3. As a Coordinator

"I'm Malek, the training coordinator at MDDRC. I handle the logistics for every session - from setting up participants to submitting training reports. I'm the busiest user in the system..."

3.1 What I See

7 tabs: Sessions (current sessions with full participant/trainer detail), Management (attendance, bulk uploads, photos), Past Training (completed sessions), Report (training report generation - DOCX/PDF), Analytics (charts and statistics), My Earnings, My Payroll.

3.2 What's Good

  • Session detail view is comprehensive: I can see all participants, their verification status, test completion, attendance, checklists - everything in one place.
  • Bulk participant upload: Excel/CSV upload for adding 20+ participants at once. Massive time saver vs one-by-one entry.
  • Training report generation: DOCX and PDF report generation with photo uploads (group photo, theory, practical). Professional output.
  • Photo management: I can upload training photos directly to each session. Good for documentation and compliance.
  • Analytics tab: Charts showing session completion rates, participant stats, test score distributions. Useful for management reporting.
  • Attendance management: Full control over marking attendance, with clock-in/out times visible.
  • Earnings and payroll visible: Same as trainer - coordinator income is transparent.

3.3 What Needs Improving

IssueImpactSuggestion
No dashboard summary / "at a glance" viewI have to click into each session to see statusAdd a dashboard card: "3 sessions this week, 2 reports pending, 45 participants total"
7 tabs still requires scrolling on mobileMy Earnings and My Payroll tabs are hidden off-screenGroup earnings/payroll into a single "Finance" tab with sub-sections
Report generation is multi-stepGenerate DOCX > Download > Edit > Re-upload > Convert to PDF > Submit - too many stepsSimplify: allow editing directly in browser, or auto-generate PDF from data without DOCX round-trip
No quick way to see incomplete tasks"Which sessions still need reports? Which participants haven't done post-test?"Add a "To-Do" or "Action Required" section showing incomplete items across all sessions
Participant names truncated in tablesMalaysian names are long (e.g., "Muhammad Nor Aiman Bin Ahmad Faizal"). Tables clip them.Use tooltips on hover or expandable rows

4. As a Supervisor (Client Contact Person)

"I'm Mr. Razak from Sapura's HSE department. My company hired MDDRC to train our 20 offshore staff. I've been given a Supervisor login to oversee the training. Here's what I see..."

4.1 What I See

4 tabs: My Sessions, Attendance, Reports, Checklists.

4.2 What's Good

  • Attendance visibility: I can see which of my staff attended, their clock-in/out times, and completion status. Good for my HR reporting.
  • Checklist verification: I can approve/reject vehicle checklists. Gives me oversight on safety compliance.
  • Training reports access: I can view and download the training report PDF. Important for DOSH/HRDC documentation.
  • Clean, simple layout: Not overwhelming. 4 tabs, easy to navigate.

No way to download attendance listI often need to submit an attendance list to my HSE manager. Currently I screenshot the table.Add "Export to Excel/PDF" button on attendance tab

The Bottom Line

Trainer and Coordinator dashboards are solid. They have the tools they need for daily work, with room for quality-of-life improvements.

Participant dashboard works but lacks guidance. A trainee shouldn't have to guess "what do I do next?" A progress tracker and training schedule would transform the experience.

Supervisor dashboard needs urgent attention. It's the client's window into MDDRC's service quality. Right now it shows 4 basic tabs with minimal functionality. The client can't see test results, can't download certificates, can't see invoices, and checklists show IDs instead of names. This is the role with the most room for improvement and the highest business impact (client retention and satisfaction).

Recommended first action: Spend 3-5 days rebuilding the Supervisor dashboard. It will have the single biggest impact on how clients perceive MDDRC's professionalism and service quality.
