1. Trainee / Participant

8.5 / 10

What's Good

  • 7-step progress tracker on Overview tab (verification > pre-test > attendance > training > post-test > feedback > certificate)
  • Schedule view showing day-by-day activities
  • Emergency contact capture during verification
  • Photo upload/camera capture during verification (just completed)
  • Pre-test and post-test taking UI, results displayed
  • Feedback form submission, certificate viewing
  • Checklist display, personal details editing in Settings

9. Auditor

6.0 / 10

What's Good

  • Audit trail tab in Finance dashboard captures all financial changes
  • Finance audit log tracks before/after values with timestamps
  • Auditor P&L tab component exists
  • Data Management edits are tracked with reasons

Needs Improvement

  • No dedicated "Auditor" role — must use admin access
  • Audit trail is within Finance tab, not a standalone view
  • Cannot filter audit trail by entity type, date range, or user
  • No export of audit trail

What's Missing

  • Dedicated auditor portal with read-only access
  • Document trail linking (invoice > payment > receipt > credit note chain)
  • Variance reports (budgeted vs actual per session)
  • Exception report (unusual amounts, late payments, voided invoices)
  • Compliance checks (missing invoices for completed sessions)
  • Exportable audit package (all financial docs for a period as ZIP)

10. Buyer of the App

8.0 / 10

What's Good

  • Comprehensive scope: 38 backend modules, 900+ endpoints, covers full training lifecycle
  • Role-based access: 7+ distinct roles with appropriate permissions
  • Financial depth: Invoice, payment, receipt, credit note, P&L, journal entries, petty cash, payables
  • Training lifecycle: From quotation to certificate, fully tracked
  • Audit compliance: Financial changes tracked with before/after values
  • Branding: Company settings, logo, invoice/receipt customization
  • Certificate verification: Public QR-based verification page
  • Data integrity: Invoice overrides auto-sync, auto-heal in PDFs
  • Modern stack: React + FastAPI + MongoDB, responsive design

Needs Improvement

  • No email integration: All notifications are in-app only. No email for invoices, receipts, certificates.
  • No WhatsApp integration: Critical for Malaysian B2B communication
  • Single-tenant: Currently one company. Multi-tenancy needed for SaaS
  • No onboarding wizard: New user setup is manual
  • Mobile experience: Functional but not optimized (tab overflow, small touch targets)

What's Missing for Market-Readiness

  • Email/WhatsApp comms: Invoice delivery, payment reminders, cert notifications
  • Certificate PDF auto-generation: Currently requires manual template. Needs engine.
  • Multi-tenancy: For selling to multiple training companies
  • Payment gateway: Stripe/FPX for online invoice payment
  • Reports export: Balance Sheet, SST Summary, HRDC reports
  • Backup/restore UI: Backend exists but no admin-facing UI
  • Dedicated Accountant/Auditor portals: Read-only access for external professionals
